## About me
I have had an interest in computers, math and statistics since I was a child. I always needed to know what is the 
tallest mountain in the area, how many people live in this village and so on. Later in life I discovered I am 
quite decent at math and that I can use the computer to get many of the numbers and statistics I wanted to know.
That however sparked another question for me. How do these computers even work ? My interest in numbers as well
as the need to discover how the computers even know what do with the numbers shaped my academic career.

These days I have graduated with honors from the Faculty of Informatics and Informational Sciences of Slovak 
Technical University in Bratislava with Bachelors degree, which will soon be followed by Masters degree focused
on data science and AI. My studies gave me much and now I am seeking new challanges to push my understanding
even further.
